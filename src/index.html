Safety Data Sheet (SDS): Coffee, brewed
1. Identification
Product: coffee, brewed, hot. Also known as: fuel, the good stuff. Recommended use: operator morale. Restrictions on use: none, but see Section 8.
2. Hazards
Signal word: WARNING. Hot: may cause burns. Contains caffeine: may cause jitters and sudden opinions about feeds and speeds. Prolonged absence causes reduced morale. The mug is not a tool holder.
3. Composition
Water, roughly 98%. Coffee, roughly 2%. Caffeine, about 95 mg per 8 oz cup (typical; real cups vary). Trace amounts of opinions.
4. First aid
Skin: cool running water. Eyes: rinse for 15 minutes and get medical help. Ingestion: intended route. Spilled on the keyboard: unplug it, do not panic, panic anyway.
5. Fire-fighting
Not flammable. A pot left on an empty hot plate is another matter: switch it off.
6. Accidental release
Wipe it up with a paper towel, not the good rags. Report anything larger than a puddle.
7. Handling and storage
Keep the beans or grounds in a sealed container, cool and dry. The coffee scoop is labelled For reference only and is not a measuring instrument.
8. Exposure controls
Daily limits by body weight are in the Caffeine Budget tab of this very room.
9. Physical properties
Appearance: black. Odor: hopeful. Temperature: hot enough to matter.
10. Disposal
Grounds go in the trash, not down the sink. Leftover coffee is a personal decision.
Sections 11 to 16
Not applicable. It is coffee.
Training Guide: the coffee maker (Bunn style)
  1. Read this guide. If the brewer is making a noise, read it again.
  2. Put the empty pot under the funnel before you start. The pot, not your mug.
  3. Put a filter in the funnel. Yes, really.
  4. Find the coffee scoop and confirm it is labelled For reference only. It is a reference tool, not a measuring instrument. Use judgment.
  5. Add coffee: one reference scoop per cup, plus one for the pot, plus the one you were going to add anyway.
  6. Add the water or press the brew button, depending on the model. Doing it more than once does not make it faster.
  7. Wait. Do not pull the pot mid-brew unless you enjoy cleaning the counter.
  8. Pour your cup, then put the pot back on the warmer.
  9. If you take the last cup, make a new pot. This is the only rule that matters.
  10. When the pot is empty, turn the warmer off and rinse the pot. Descale the brewer on the manufacturer's schedule.
